# Resume bullets

- Designed and ran, with coding agents, an autonomous Runge-Kutta coefficient search in Docker (Python, PowerShell). A language model steers each search cycle through a JSON directive that can only narrow it, and no person picks candidates or scores. Epoch 1 wrote 141,364 scored records over 3,931 search cycles. (claims R12, R13)
- Kept the scorer out of the search's reach: a read-only harness mount, a sha256 pin over the scoring files (10 in epoch 1, 14 in epoch 2) checked at every start and stored in every record, 91 golden and canary cases, and 1,908 tests as of 2026-09-23. (claim R11)
- Found a three-stage dyadic method with 2.95x lower held-out error than midpoint, the best of eight classical methods, in Q15 with floor rounding at a 65,536-cycle budget, on the epoch-1 analytic cost model with magnitude weighting. The figure carries selection bias and has not been re-measured under the corrected cost model; on traced whole-step cost it falls to 1.01x, which dropping one held-out problem overturns. In float64, rk4 is 1,992x to 420,975x more accurate. (claims R1, R5)
- Ran a host-side audit that compiled the integrator step with GCC 13.2.1 and traced it on an emulator that is instruction-accurate but not cycle-accurate. It matched the Python evaluator in 144 of 144 cases and caught the cost model ranking rk4 and rk38 backwards, and the run froze epoch 1 rather than rescore it. (claims R7, R12)
- Tested three discovered methods on eight out-of-sample problems chosen after the search began. The best of them had lower Q15 error than the best of five classical methods run on 4 of 5 non-stiff problems, and the fixed champion by itself on 3. On the stiff `robertson_scaled`, every discovered method overflowed, as did rk4 and rk38. (claim R4)
- Built a Runge-Kutta evaluation harness in Python in 2025 (explicit Butcher-table stepper, ODE generator, SciPy reference solver) and ran it over 10,000 generated ODEs. Audited it in September 2026 and published corrections: evolution was seeded with RK4 and Dormand-Prince under a score clipped at 1, so the claimed rediscovery failed, and the generator was never trained. (claims N9, N1, N2, N4)
