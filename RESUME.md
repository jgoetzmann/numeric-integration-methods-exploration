# Resume bullets

Bullets for a CV, true as of the snapshot on 2026-09-23. Each one ends with the claims that back it in the audit at https://jgoetzmann.github.io/numeric-integration-methods-exploration/claims.html, where every claim carries its verdict, limits and evidence. Quote a number with the conditions beside it.

- Designed and ran, with coding agents, an autonomous Runge-Kutta coefficient search in Docker (Python, PowerShell): a language model steers each search cycle through a JSON directive that can only narrow it, and no person picks candidates or scores. Epoch 1 wrote 141,364 scored records over 3,931 search cycles. (claims R12, R13)
- Guarded the scorer from the search: a read-only harness mount, a sha256 pin over the scoring files (10 in epoch 1, 14 in epoch 2) checked at every start and stored in every record, 91 golden and canary cases, and 1,908 tests as of 2026-09-23. (claim R11)
- Found a three-stage dyadic method with 2.95x lower held-out error than midpoint, the best of eight classical methods, in Q15 with floor rounding at a 65,536-cycle budget, on the epoch-1 analytic cost model with magnitude weighting (selection bias; not yet re-measured under the corrected model; 1.01x on traced cost). In float64, rk4 is 1,992x to 420,975x more accurate. (claims R1, R5)
- Ran a host-side audit that compiled the integrator step with GCC 13.2.1 and traced it on an instruction-accurate, not cycle-accurate, emulator. It matched the Python evaluator in 144 of 144 cases and caught the cost model ranking rk4 and rk38 backwards. Froze epoch 1 rather than rescore it. (claims R7, R12)
- Tested three discovered methods on eight out-of-sample problems chosen after the search began: the best of them on each problem had lower Q15 error than the best classical method on 4 of 5 non-stiff problems, the fixed champion on 3. On the stiff `robertson_scaled`, every discovered method overflowed, as did rk4 and rk38. (claim R4)
- Built a Runge-Kutta evaluation harness in Python in 2025 (explicit Butcher-table stepper, ODE generator, SciPy reference solver) and ran it over 10,000 generated ODEs. Audited it in September 2026 and published corrections: evolution was seeded with RK4 and Dormand-Prince under a score clipped at 1, so the claimed rediscovery failed, and the generator was never trained. (claims N9, N1, N2, N4)
